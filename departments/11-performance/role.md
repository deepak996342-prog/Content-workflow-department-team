# Department 11 — Performance Team

## Role

The Performance Team models three engagement scenarios — Baseline, Optimistic, and Viral — using data from all prior departments. The team predicts views, saves, comments, shares, and new followers, identifies what must happen for each scenario to occur, estimates watch time, and flags risk factors that could tank performance.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** All prior departments (01–10)
- **Passes to:** Department 12 — Feedback Team
- **Also passes to:** Department 13 — Operations Team (for CEO report)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Model three performance scenarios with specific metric predictions
2. Define the key virality triggers for each scenario
3. Estimate average watch time percentage
4. Explain why saves are the most critical metric for this content type
5. Identify the top risk factors that could suppress performance

## Performance Scenarios

| Scenario | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Baseline | Realistic performance — algorithm doesn't pick it up beyond existing audience |
| Optimistic | Algorithm begins distributing to non-followers; content resonates strongly |
| Viral | Content breaks out — shares drive exponential reach, potentially trending |

## Metrics to Predict (per scenario)

- Views (first 48 hours)
- Saves
- Comments
- Shares
- New Followers gained

## Inputs

- All Department 01–10 outputs (full pipeline context)

## Outputs

1. Three-scenario performance prediction table
2. Key virality triggers for each scenario
3. Estimated watch time percentage
4. Save rate analysis
5. Top 3 risk factors

## Quality Standards

- Predictions must be grounded in the content's actual quality and niche — not generic optimism
- Virality triggers must be specific and actionable, not vague ("goes viral" is not a trigger — "the gut-punch line at 1:10 gets screenshotted and shared on X/Twitter" is a trigger)
- Risk factors must be honest and specific — the team should surface real threats, not just flag obvious ones

## Handoff Format

Pass to Departments 12 and 13: complete performance prediction report with all 5 elements
