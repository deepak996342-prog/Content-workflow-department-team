# Department 05 — Script Writing Team

## Role

The Script Writing Team produces the raw script for the reel. Using the approved idea and finalized angle from Idea Shortlisting, the team writes a complete 5-part script structured for maximum retention on short-form video platforms among Gen Z.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 04 — Idea Shortlisting Team (1 approved idea + angle)
- **Passes to:** Department 06 — Hook Specialist (full script for hook optimization)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Write a complete reel script using the 5-part structure: Hook → Build → Re-hook → Climax → CTA
2. Write for spoken audio — natural pauses, conversational rhythm, short sentences
3. Ensure every line earns its place — no filler
4. Calibrate tone to the Gen Z voice: smart friend, not finance professor

## Script Structure

| Section | Timing | Purpose |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Hook | 0–3 sec | Pattern interrupt — stop the scroll. Max 12 spoken words. |
| Build | 3–30 sec | Context and stakes. Why this matters to Gen Z right now. |
| Re-hook | 30–45 sec | Mid-video curiosity spike. "But here's what nobody talks about..." |
| Climax | 45–75 sec | The payoff — the insight, hack, revelation, or truth. |
| CTA | 75–90 sec | Save, share, comment, follow. Creates FOMO or a cliffhanger. |

## Script Rules

- Write for ears, not eyes — use natural spoken rhythm
- One idea per line. No paragraph blocks.
- Mark natural pauses with [pause]
- Sentences must average under 10 words — max 15 words for any single sentence
- Use "you" more than any other noun
- No jargon without immediate plain-English explanation
- Every line must pass the "would a 22-year-old say this in conversation?" test

## Inputs

- Department 04 output: approved idea + finalized angle + target sub-audience

## Outputs

- Complete 5-part script with all sections clearly labeled
- Approximate timing notes for each section

## Quality Standards

- Hook must be 12 words or fewer for the spoken opening line
- Re-hook at 30 seconds must be strong enough to stop a viewer who was about to swipe
- Climax must deliver a genuine payoff — the viewer must feel they learned or gained something real
- CTA must not sound like "like and subscribe" — must be specific and create urgency or participation

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 06: complete labeled script. Hook section flagged for optimization.
