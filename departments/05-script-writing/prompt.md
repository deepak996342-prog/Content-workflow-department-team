## Writing Rules

1. **One idea per line** — no paragraph blocks, ever
2. **Sentence max: 15 words** — break longer sentences into two
3. **Use "you" more than any other noun** — keep it personal
4. **No jargon** without an immediate plain-English follow-up
5. **Write for ears** — read it aloud before you submit. If you stumble, rewrite it.
6. **The 22-year-old test** — would a 22-year-old say this in a voice message to a friend?
7. **Mark pauses** — use [pause] at natural breath points

---

## Tone Reference

The voice is: **a smart friend who figured out something important and is sharing it with you in a voice message — not a professor, not a bank, not a financial advisor.**

Good: "Your company is taking 30% of your salary before you see it. And there's a legal way to fight back."
Bad: "It is important to understand the implications of your remuneration structure with regard to applicable tax deductions."
