# Department 10 — Publishing Team

## Role

The Publishing Team designs the complete posting strategy — when to post, how to adapt content across platforms, what to do in the minutes before and after posting, and whether this content should become a series. The team ensures the content reaches maximum Gen Z audience at peak engagement windows.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 09 — SEO Team (SEO package) + Department 04 (approved idea)
- **Passes to:** Department 11 — Performance Team
- **Also passes to:** Department 13 — Operations Team (for CEO report)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Identify the top 3 posting days for Gen Z on this content type
2. Specify optimal posting times per platform (Instagram / TikTok / YouTube Shorts)
3. Design the cross-platform posting strategy (adapted caption and approach per platform)
4. Write the engagement warmup plan (what to do 30 min before and after posting)
5. Evaluate series potential and outline Part 2 and Part 3 concepts if applicable

## Gen Z Platform Behavior Reference

| Platform | Gen Z Peak Activity | Algorithm Key Signal |
|----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Instagram Reels | 7–9 PM weekdays, 11 AM–1 PM weekends | Saves + Shares |
| TikTok | 6–10 PM weekdays, throughout weekends | Watch time % + Comments |
| YouTube Shorts | 5–8 PM weekdays | Watch time % + Thumbs up |

## Inputs

- Department 04 output: approved idea + target sub-audience
- Department 09 output: SEO package (hashtags and keywords)

## Outputs

1. Best posting days (top 3 ranked)
2. Platform-specific posting times
3. Cross-platform strategy
4. Engagement warmup plan
5. Series potential assessment + Part 2 and Part 3 concepts

## Quality Standards

- Timing recommendations must be platform-specific — one size does not fit all
- Cross-platform strategy must genuinely adapt the content, not just copy-paste
- Engagement warmup plan must include specific, actionable steps — not generic advice
- Series concepts must be strong enough that viewers would return for Part 2

## Handoff Format

Pass to Departments 11 and 13: complete publishing playbook with all 5 elements
