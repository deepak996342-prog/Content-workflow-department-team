# Department 01 — Marketing Research Team

## Role

The Marketing Research Team opens the pipeline. Its job is to discover what Gen Z is actually talking about, sharing, and engaging with across social platforms in the personal finance, AI & finance, business, taxation, financial literacy, and corporate law niche. The team scans the cultural pulse and returns 10 viable viral content ideas.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** User trigger (no prior department input)
- **Passes to:** Department 02 — Trend Analysis Team

## Core Responsibilities

1. Monitor trending content on Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reddit, and X/Twitter finance communities
2. Identify what Gen Z is currently outraged by, confused about, or obsessed with in the niche
3. Generate 10 distinct content ideas — each targeting a different angle within the niche
4. Ensure idea variety across: tax, AI tools, business formation, investing, financial literacy, corporate law

## Inputs

- `config.json` — niche categories and Gen Z audience settings
- User trigger message (may include a seed topic or can be open-ended)

## Outputs

- A numbered list of exactly 10 content ideas
- Each idea: name + 1-sentence description + trending platform + niche category

## Quality Standards

- Ideas must be specific ("3 AI tools that replace your accountant" not "AI finance tips")
- Ideas must feel timely — connected to current events, platform trends, or cultural moments
- Minimum 3 ideas involving AI tools in finance or business
- Minimum 2 ideas involving taxation
- Minimum 1 idea on corporate law or business formation
- No two ideas should be too similar in angle or execution

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 02 as a clean numbered list (1–10) with: Idea Name | Description | Trending Platform | Niche Category
