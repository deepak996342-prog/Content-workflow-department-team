# Department 03 — Content Strategy Team

## Role

The Content Strategy Team takes the top 3 ranked ideas and transforms each one from a raw concept into a fully defined strategic brief. The team defines the angle, target sub-audience within Gen Z, emotional trigger, and content pillar for each idea — giving the Idea Shortlisting Team everything it needs to pick the winner.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 02 — Trend Analysis Team (top 3 ranked ideas)
- **Passes to:** Department 04 — Idea Shortlisting Team (3 strategy briefs)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Define the strategic angle for each of the top 3 ideas
2. Identify the specific Gen Z sub-audience each idea speaks to most
3. Map the core emotional trigger (curiosity, outrage, FOMO, pride, fear, hope)
4. Assign a content pillar (Educate / Entertain / Inspire / Warn)
5. Validate that each idea fits the niche and Gen Z audience from `config.json`

## Inputs

- Department 02 output: top 3 ranked ideas with scores and rationale

## Outputs

- 3 fully defined content strategy briefs, one per idea

## Strategy Brief Structure

Each brief must contain:
- **Idea Name**
- **Angle:** The specific lens or perspective to approach the content from
- **Target Sub-Audience:** The subset of Gen Z this speaks to most (e.g., first-jobbers aged 22–25, college students, Gen Z entrepreneurs, side-hustle crowd, crypto-curious)
- **Emotional Trigger:** The primary emotion the content activates
- **Content Pillar:** Educate / Entertain / Inspire / Warn
- **Why This Will Work:** 2 sentences on why this angle is right for Gen Z right now

## Quality Standards

- Each brief must have a distinct angle — no two briefs should feel like the same content
- Angles must be specific enough that a scriptwriter can begin immediately
- Emotional triggers must be genuine — "hope" requires an aspiration payoff, "outrage" requires a genuine injustice
- The Sub-Audience must be a real Gen Z persona, not a generic description

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 04: 3 labeled strategy briefs in full detail
