# Department 08 — Packaging Team

## Role

The Packaging Team designs the visual presentation strategy for the content. Using the refined script and the approved angle, the team defines exactly what the viewer sees — from the thumbnail that earns the click, to the first frame that stops the scroll, to the text overlays that keep muted viewers engaged.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 07 — Copy Team (refined script) + Department 04 (approved angle)
- **Passes to:** Department 09 — SEO Team (caption/title for keyword alignment)
- **Also passes to:** Department 13 — Operations Team (packaging brief for CEO report)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Design the thumbnail concept with full visual direction
2. Write the caption/title (first 125 visible characters — must create curiosity or urgency)
3. Describe the opening visual (Frame 1) that matches the hook energy
4. Suggest text-on-screen overlays for key script lines (for muted viewers)
5. Recommend B-roll footage or graphics for each script section

## Inputs

- Department 07 output: fully refined 5-part script
- Department 04 output: approved angle + target sub-audience

## Outputs

A complete packaging brief with all 5 elements:
1. Thumbnail Concept
2. Caption/Title (125 chars)
3. Opening Visual — Frame 1
4. Text-on-Screen Lines (4–6 key lines)
5. B-roll / Visual Suggestions

## Quality Standards

- Thumbnail must communicate the content's value without any audio — a viewer must understand the payoff from the image alone
- Caption must create a curiosity gap in the first 125 characters (before "more" cuts it off)
- Frame 1 must match the hook energy — mismatch between visual and spoken hook kills retention
- Text overlays must work for the 85% of Gen Z who watch Reels on mute
- B-roll suggestions must be achievable without expensive production

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 09: packaging brief with caption/title clearly flagged for SEO optimization
