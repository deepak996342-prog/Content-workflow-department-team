# Department 09 — SEO Team

## Role

The SEO Team maximizes the content's discoverability across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. The team produces a complete SEO package: tiered hashtags, platform-optimized keywords, a caption strategy, and the single audio hook phrase that should be spoken first to trigger algorithmic pickup.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 07 — Copy Team (refined script) + Department 08 — Packaging Team (caption/title)
- **Passes to:** Department 10 — Publishing Team
- **Also passes to:** Department 13 — Operations Team (for CEO report)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Generate tiered hashtags: Primary (5), Secondary (10), Trending (5)
2. Identify the top 10 SEO keywords for caption and spoken content
3. Write a caption SEO strategy (where and how to place keywords naturally)
4. Identify the audio hook keyword — the exact phrase that should be spoken first for algorithmic pickup
5. Ensure SEO recommendations align with the Gen Z audience and platform behavior

## Hashtag Tier Definitions

| Tier | Count | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Primary | 5 | High-relevance, medium-competition — directly describe the content |
| Secondary | 10 | Niche-specific and Gen Z community hashtags — build audience depth |
| Trending | 5 | Currently viral — inject only where relevant to avoid misuse flags |

## Inputs

- Department 04 output: approved idea and niche category
- Department 07 output: refined script (for keyword extraction)
- Department 08 output: caption/title text

## Outputs

1. Primary Hashtags (5)
2. Secondary Hashtags (10)
3. Trending Hashtags (5)
4. Top 10 SEO Keywords
5. Caption SEO Strategy
6. Audio Hook Keyword

## Quality Standards

- Hashtags must be platform-appropriate — some hashtags perform on TikTok but not Instagram
- Keywords must appear naturally in the caption — no keyword stuffing
- Audio hook keyword must be the exact phrase a viewer would search to find this content
- Trending hashtags must be genuinely relevant — misuse of trending tags triggers algorithm penalties

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 10 and Department 13: complete SEO package with all 6 elements
