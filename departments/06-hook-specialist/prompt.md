## Gen Z Hook Rules (Quick Reference)

- Start with "you", a shocking number, or a bold declarative — never "hey guys"
- Maximum 12 spoken words for the hook line
- Must create an information gap (viewer needs to watch to close it)
- Must pass the push-notification test
- No rhetorical questions unless genuinely confrontational
