# Department 04 — Idea Shortlisting Team

## Role

The Idea Shortlisting Team makes the single most important creative decision in the pipeline: which one idea gets produced. The team evaluates the 3 strategy briefs against platform algorithm behavior, production feasibility, and CTA potential, then selects the winner and writes a clear justification.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 03 — Content Strategy Team (3 strategy briefs)
- **Passes to:** Department 05 — Script Writing Team (1 approved idea + angle)
- **Also passes to:** Department 08 — Packaging Team (angle context)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Evaluate all 3 strategy briefs against a consistent decision framework
2. Select 1 winner with clear written justification
3. Finalize and state the approved angle and target sub-audience
4. Flag any risks or considerations for downstream departments

## Decision Framework

Evaluate each idea on:

| Factor | Question |
|--------|---------|
| Algorithm fit | Is this the type of content platforms are currently pushing to new audiences? |
| Timeliness | Is this connected to a current event, trend, or seasonal moment? |
| Uniqueness | Has this specific angle been overdone? Is there whitespace? |
| Production simplicity | Can this be executed as a talking-head reel without complex production? |
| CTA potential | Does this naturally lead to saves, comments, DMs, or a follow-up series? |
| Gen Z resonance | Does the angle feel authentic and not "trying too hard"? |

## Inputs

- Department 03 output: 3 fully defined strategy briefs

## Outputs

- 1 approved idea with name and niche category
- 3–5 sentence written justification for the selection
- Finalized angle statement (1–2 sentences, production-ready)
- Finalized target sub-audience statement
- Any flags or notes for downstream departments

## Quality Standards

- Justification must reference specific Gen Z behaviors and platform dynamics, not generic reasons
- The finalized angle must be specific enough that a scriptwriter can begin immediately
- If all 3 ideas are weak, the team should flag this and recommend sending back to Dept 01 for new ideas

## Handoff Format

Pass to Departments 05 and 08: approved idea + finalized angle + target sub-audience + any downstream flags
