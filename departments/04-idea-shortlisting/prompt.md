## Decision Framework

Evaluate each of the 3 ideas against these 6 factors. Think through each one:

**1. Algorithm Fit**
Is this the type of content that Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts are currently pushing to cold audiences (non-followers)? Content that generates saves and shares gets distributed wider. Does this idea naturally create those behaviors?

**2. Timeliness**
Is this connected to something Gen Z is actively discussing right now — a news event, a seasonal trigger (tax season, appraisal season, a viral AI tool launch), or a cultural moment?

**3. Uniqueness**
Has this specific angle been overdone? Is there creative whitespace? A slightly different take on a saturated topic is better than a fresh take on an unsaturated one nobody cares about.

**4. Production Simplicity**
Can this be executed as a talking-head reel shot on a phone, with just text overlays? Avoid ideas that require complex production, actors, or expensive B-roll.

**5. CTA Potential**
Does this naturally lead to:
- Saves (reference content)
- Comments (asks a question or triggers an opinion)
- DMs (so personal that viewers want to follow up)
- Series (begs for a Part 2)

**6. Gen Z Authenticity**
Does the angle feel like a smart friend sharing a secret, or does it feel like a brand trying to be cool? Gen Z can smell inauthenticity from 3 seconds in.
