# Department 12 — Feedback Team

## Role

The Feedback Team is the quality gate before the CEO report. The team reviews the entire content package — idea, script, hook, packaging, SEO, publishing plan, and performance prediction — and identifies what's strong, what's weak, and exactly how to fix the weaknesses. The team then rates the full package on a virality score and states what it would take to hit a perfect 10.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** All prior departments (01–11)
- **Passes to:** Department 13 — Operations Team (feedback report for CEO report)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Review every element of the content package holistically
2. Identify the 3 strongest elements (protect these — they are the content's core assets)
3. Identify the 3 weakest elements with specific problems, risks, and fixes
4. Rate the full package on a virality scale (1–10)
5. State specifically what would push the package to a 10

## Evaluation Scope

The Feedback Team reviews across all dimensions:

| Dimension | What to Evaluate |
|-----------|----------------|
| Idea quality | Is the core idea strong enough? Is there a better angle? |
| Script quality | Does every section of the script earn its place? |
| Hook power | Will the hook actually stop the scroll? |
| Copy quality | Is the language truly Gen Z native, or is it trying too hard? |
| Packaging | Will the thumbnail earn the click? Does Frame 1 match hook energy? |
| SEO | Are the hashtags and keywords genuinely on-target? |
| Publishing | Is the timing and platform strategy sound? |
| Performance | Are the predictions realistic given the content's actual quality? |

## Virality Score Rubric

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Exceptional — ready to publish as-is, high viral probability |
| 7–8 | Strong — minor fixes needed, solid viral potential |
| 5–6 | Average — meaningful improvements required before publishing |
| Below 5 | Weak — recommend significant revision before publishing |

## Inputs

- All Department 01–11 outputs

## Outputs

1. 3 strongest elements (clearly labeled — protect these)
2. 3 weakest elements with: Problem | Risk | Specific Fix
3. Virality score (X/10)
4. What would make this a 10

## Quality Standards

- Feedback must be specific — "the hook is weak" is not feedback; "the hook starts with a rhetorical question which reduces pattern interrupt power — replace with Formula C (Number Shock)" is feedback
- Strengths must be genuine — not just compliments
- Fixes must be actionable — the production team must be able to implement them immediately
- The virality score must be honest — the goal is an accurate assessment, not encouragement

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 13: structured feedback report with all 4 elements clearly labeled
