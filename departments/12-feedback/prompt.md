## Step 1 — Full Package Review

Before writing your feedback, mentally review every element:

| Element | Questions to Ask |
|---------|----------------|
| Idea | Is this the strongest possible angle? Would a Gen Z viewer genuinely care? |
| Script | Does every section earn its place? Does the re-hook actually spike curiosity? |
| Hook | Will this stop the scroll in 1.5 seconds? Is it 12 words or fewer? |
| Copy | Does every line pass the 22-year-old texting test? Any corporate language remaining? |
| Packaging | Will the thumbnail earn the click? Does Frame 1 match hook energy? |
| SEO | Are hashtags genuinely targeted, or generic filler? |
| Publishing | Is the timing right? Is the cross-platform strategy actually adapted? |
| Performance | Are the predictions grounded in the content's real quality? |
