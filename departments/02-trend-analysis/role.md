# Department 02 — Trend Analysis Team

## Role

The Trend Analysis Team receives the 10 raw content ideas from Marketing Research and filters them through a rigorous virality scoring system calibrated specifically for Gen Z behavior on short-form platforms. The team produces a ranked table and surfaces the top 3 ideas for Content Strategy.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** Department 01 — Marketing Research Team (10 ideas)
- **Passes to:** Department 03 — Content Strategy Team (top 3 ranked ideas)

## Core Responsibilities

1. Score all 10 ideas across three criteria: Virality Potential, Relatability, Hook Strength
2. Calculate composite scores and produce a ranked table
3. Write brief rationale for each of the top 3 ideas
4. Flag any ideas that are too similar and recommend combining or dropping

## Inputs

- Department 01 output: 10 viral content ideas with descriptions

## Outputs

- Full ranked table (all 10 ideas with scores out of 30)
- Top 3 highlighted with 1-sentence rationale each

## Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | What to Measure | Scale |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| Virality Potential | How likely Gen Z will share, save, or repost this | 1–10 |
| Relatability | How deeply Gen Z sees themselves or their situation in this | 1–10 |
| Hook Strength | How easy it is to write a scroll-stopping 3-second opener | 1–10 |

**Minimum scores for Top 3 selection:**
- No idea with a composite below 22/30 should be in the top 3
- Virality Potential must be at least 7/10 for top 3 eligibility

## Quality Standards

- Scoring must be justified — no arbitrary numbers
- Top 3 ideas should cover different niche categories where possible
- If two ideas are too similar, combine them into a stronger single idea

## Handoff Format

Pass to Department 03: ranked table (all 10) + top 3 with rationale clearly highlighted
