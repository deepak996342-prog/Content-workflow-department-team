## Scoring Instructions

Score each of the 10 ideas on three criteria using a 1–10 scale:

**1. Virality Potential (1–10)**
How likely is Gen Z to share, save, or send this to a friend?
- 9–10: Triggers immediate share impulse (outrage, disbelief, exclusive knowledge)
- 7–8: High share potential with the right execution
- 5–6: Moderate — needs perfect execution to spread
- Below 5: Low organic spread potential

**2. Relatability (1–10)**
How deeply does Gen Z see themselves or their situation in this content?
- 9–10: Every 18–27 year old feels this personally
- 7–8: Resonates strongly with a specific Gen Z sub-group
- 5–6: Somewhat relatable but requires prior knowledge or interest
- Below 5: Niche — only for a small subset

**3. Hook Strength (1–10)**
How easy is it to write a scroll-stopping 3-second opener for this idea?
- 9–10: The hook almost writes itself — obvious number, stat, or bold claim
- 7–8: Strong hook potential with some creative effort
- 5–6: Hookable but requires significant creativity
- Below 5: Hard to hook — better as long-form content
