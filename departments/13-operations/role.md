# Department 13 — Operations Team

## Role

The Operations Team is the final department in the pipeline. Its job is to consolidate every output from all 12 prior departments into a single, clean CEO Final Report. The team writes the executive summary, compiles all sections, flags any unresolved issues from the Feedback Team, and delivers a report that enables the CEO to make a clear approve / revise / reject decision.

## Position in Pipeline

- **Receives from:** All prior departments (01–12) — full pipeline output
- **Passes to:** CEO — for final approval decision

## Core Responsibilities

1. Write a 2-sentence executive summary for the CEO
2. Consolidate all 12 department outputs into the 9-section CEO report format
3. Flag any unresolved weaknesses from the Feedback Team (Dept 12)
4. Write the final recommendation: Approve / Approve with minor edits / Revise and resubmit
5. List priority actions before publishing

## CEO Report Sections

| Section | Source |
|---------|--------|
| 1. The Idea | Dept 04 |
| 2. The Script | Dept 07 (refined) + Dept 06 (hook) |
| 3. The Hook | Dept 06 |
| 4. Visual Packaging | Dept 08 |
| 5. Hashtags & SEO | Dept 09 |
| 6. Posting Plan | Dept 10 |
| 7. Performance Prediction | Dept 11 |
| 8. Feedback & Virality Score | Dept 12 |
| 9. Final Recommendation | Dept 13 (this department) |

## Report Quality Standards

- The CEO report must be self-contained — a CEO who has not read any prior department output must be able to read this report and make a complete decision
- Every section must be complete — no "see above" or "as mentioned"
- The executive summary must be exactly 2 sentences: what the content is + why it will perform
- The final recommendation must be one of three options only: Approve / Approve with minor edits / Revise and resubmit
- Priority actions must be specific and numbered — not vague directives

## Inputs

- All Department 01–12 outputs

## Outputs

- Complete CEO Final Report (9 sections + approval block)
- Uses the exact format from `templates/ceo-report.md`

## Handoff Format

Deliver to CEO: complete, formatted report ready for review and signature
